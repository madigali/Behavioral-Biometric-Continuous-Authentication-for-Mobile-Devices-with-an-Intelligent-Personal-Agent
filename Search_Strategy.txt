Search Strategy for the Systematic Review
------------------------------------------
Title: Behavioral Biometric Continuous Authentication for Mobile Devices
       with an Intelligent Personal Agent: A Systematic Review

Manuscript ID: technologies-4398074
Journal: Technologies (ISSN 2227-7080)

This systematic review was conducted and reported in accordance with
the PRISMA 2020 guidelines. This document accompanies Section 3.1
("Keyword Selection and Search Criteria") and Section 4.2 ("Publication
Trends", Figure 6, PRISMA flow diagram) of the main manuscript and
provides full per-database, per-string detail not reproduced in the
main text for length.

========================================================================
ADDITIONAL SOURCES (SUPPLEMENTARY TO DATABASE SEARCH)
========================================================================

- Backward snowballing: reference lists of 12 key included reviews
  were manually checked for additional candidates (completed
  February 2026). This step recovered one additional study published
  in 2019 that fell outside the primary 2017-2025 search window
  (see Section 4.2 of the main manuscript).
- Forward citation tracking via Google Scholar for 5 seminal papers
  (completed March 2026).
- Newly identified records from both supplementary sources were
  subjected to the same screening and quality-assessment pipeline
  (Section 3.2) as database-derived records.

========================================================================
SCREENING AND DATA MANAGEMENT
========================================================================

Retrieved records were exported in RIS format and managed using
Zotero. Deduplication was performed automatically within Zotero,
followed by manual verification. Title/abstract screening and
full-text eligibility assessment were each conducted independently
by two reviewers using a standardized eligibility checklist and the
explicit inclusion/exclusion criteria reported in Section 3.2 of the
main manuscript. Disagreements were resolved by discussion; unresolved
cases were adjudicated by a third reviewer.

========================================================================
QUALITY ASSESSMENT
========================================================================

Methodological quality of all 80 included studies was assessed using
the five-criterion, 0-2-point scoring rubric reported in full in
Section 3.2 of the main manuscript (maximum score 10 per study;
criteria 1 and 2 act as gatekeeping criteria). Of the 80 included
works, 53 are primary empirical or system-level studies; the
remaining 27 are themselves systematic reviews, narrative surveys, or
scoping reviews retained for taxonomy construction and methodological
precedent rather than for pooled performance metrics (see Section 3.2
for the full citation list and rationale).

========================================================================
KNOWN LIMITATIONS OF THIS SEARCH PROTOCOL
========================================================================

1. Keyword scope: The five search strings above were deliberately
   scoped to a constrained set of core terms and their British/
   American spelling variants (e.g., "behavioural"/"behavioral")
   rather than an exhaustive synonym list, to preserve precision given
   the breadth of themes covered. Broader synonyms that were
   considered but not included include "implicit authentication",
   "active authentication", "passive authentication", "continuous
   verification", and "voice assistant". Their omission may have
   excluded a small number of relevant records indexed only under
   those terms.

2. Search field scope: All search strings, across all six databases,
   were applied to title, abstract, and keyword fields only (TI/AB/KW);
   full-text search was not performed. This may exclude studies in
   which terms central to this review (e.g., "intelligent personal
   agent" or "behavioral biometric continuous authentication") appear
   primarily in the body text rather than in the title, abstract, or
   keywords.

Both limitations are disclosed here, and in Section 3.1 of the main
manuscript, as constraints on recall rather than retroactively
corrected, since addressing them would require re-executing the
search under a revised protocol.
