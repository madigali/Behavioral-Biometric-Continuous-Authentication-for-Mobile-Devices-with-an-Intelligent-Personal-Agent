# Screening Log

**Manuscript:** Behavioral Biometric Continuous Authentication for Mobile Devices with an Intelligent Personal Agent: A Systematic Review
**Manuscript ID:** technologies-4398074
**Journal:** Technologies (ISSN 2227-7080)

This log accompanies Section 3.1–3.3 of the main manuscript and the
Supplementary Materials file `Search_Strategy.txt`. It records the
aggregate counts at each PRISMA 2020 stage, as reported in the
manuscript (Figure 6) and in `Search_Strategy.txt`.

> **Note on granularity.** This log reports stage-level aggregate
> counts only. Per-record screening decisions (i.e., which specific
> title/abstract or full-text record was excluded, and why, at the
> level of an individual citation) were not retained as a structured,
> exportable dataset during the review process and are therefore not
> reproduced here. The aggregate counts below are fully consistent
> with, and traceable to, the numbers reported in the manuscript and
> in `Search_Strategy.txt`. Readers seeking the rationale for specific
> exclusion categories should refer to Section 3.2 of the main
> manuscript (worked exclusion examples) and to the inclusion/exclusion
> criteria stated there.

---

## 3. Title/Abstract Screening

| Stage | Count |
|---|---|
| Records screened | 380 |
| Records excluded at title/abstract screening | 70 |
| Records advancing to full-text eligibility assessment | 310 |

Screening was conducted independently by two reviewers against the
inclusion/exclusion criteria in Section 3.2 of the main manuscript;
disagreements were resolved by discussion, with a third reviewer
adjudicating unresolved cases (Section 3.3). A per-record list of the
70 excluded titles/abstracts, with an individual exclusion reason for
each, is not available as a separate exportable file; the criteria
applied are stated in full in Section 3.2.

| Stage | Count |
|---|---|

A per-record list of the 230 full-text exclusions, individually
attributed to one of the three reasons above, is not available as a
separate exportable file. Three worked examples of full-text exclusion
decisions (a blockchain-based IoT authentication architecture excluded
for lacking a behavioral biometric modality; a privacy-attitudes
survey excluded for lacking any system-level or performance-oriented
contribution; a position paper on continuous-sensing ethics excluded
for lacking empirical or system-level grounding) are documented in
full in Section 3.2 of the main manuscript.

## 5. Supplementary Identification (Snowballing)

| Method | Scope | Completion date | Yield |
|---|---|---|---|
| Backward snowballing | Reference lists of 12 key included reviews | February 2026 | 1 additional study (published 2019, outside the primary 2017–2025 search window) |
| Forward citation tracking | Google Scholar, 5 seminal papers | March 2026 | Newly identified records subjected to the same screening and quality-assessment pipeline as database-derived records |

The specific identities of the 12 reviews checked for backward
snowballing and the 5 seminal papers used for forward citation
tracking are not separately itemized in this log; they are a subset
of the 80 included studies referenced throughout the manuscript.
