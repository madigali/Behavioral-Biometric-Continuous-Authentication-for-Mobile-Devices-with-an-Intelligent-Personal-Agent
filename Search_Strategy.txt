Search Strategy for the Systematic Review
------------------------------------------
Title: Behavioral Biometric Continuous Authentication for Mobile Devices
        with an Intelligent Personal Agent: A Systematic Review

This systematic review was conducted and reported in accordance with
the PRISMA 2020 guidelines.

========================================================================
DATA MANAGEMENT
========================================================================

Retrieved records were exported in RIS format and managed using
Zotero. Deduplication was performed automatically within Zotero,
followed by manual verification. Screening was conducted by two
independent reviewers using a standardized eligibility checklist.
Disagreements were resolved by discussion; unresolved cases were
adjudicated by a third reviewer.
